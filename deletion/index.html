Data deletion guide

Use PawFolio’s in-app reset when you want to remove locally stored PawFolio data from this device.

Warning: Resetting data is destructive. Local PawFolio records and settings may not be recoverable after the reset. Review the confirmation in the app before continuing.

Delete local PawFolio data

  1. Open PawFolio and go to Settings.
  2. Open Storage (the Data Management screen).
  3. Choose Reset Settings and Data.
  4. Complete the biometric or confirmation step if requested.
  5. Allow PawFolio to finish and restart when prompted.

What the reset removes

The reset is intended to remove the app’s local feature database, preferences and settings, notification requests, image and URL caches, temporary files, documents, and PawFolio app-group files. It also removes local synced data through the app’s storage reset path.

What it does not control

The reset does not control information you separately store in Apple Photos, Calendar, Music, WeatherKit, or other Apple services. If iCloud Sync was enabled, copies may already exist in iCloud or on another device. Disabling sync stops future PawFolio synchronization from this device, but the current app does not provide a separate remote iCloud deletion control.

For help understanding an iCloud account change or synced copies, first review your Apple iCloud settings and then contact us. PawFolio does not have a separate PawFolio account or a PawFolio-hosted account database to delete.

Image cache only

If you only want to remove downloaded image caches, use Settings → Storage → Delete Image Cache. This does not delete your journal records or the original images stored elsewhere.
