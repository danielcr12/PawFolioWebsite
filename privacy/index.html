Privacy policy

PawFolio is a journal-first pet memory app. This policy explains what the app stores and how optional Apple services are involved.

What PawFolio stores

You can create pet profiles, journal entries, photos, notes, milestones, routines, reminders, and optional care details such as mood, weight, water, medications, health records, expenses, and contacts. PawFolio is designed to work without a PawFolio account.

By default, this feature data is stored in PawFolio’s local app storage on your device. PawFolio does not receive or operate a separate account database for this information.

Optional iCloud sync

If you explicitly enable Sync with iCloud, supported PawFolio data is synchronized using Apple’s iCloud services so it can be available on devices using the same iCloud account. Apple controls iCloud account infrastructure and its handling of iCloud data under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Turning sync off stops PawFolio from continuing to synchronize through the app; it does not by itself guarantee that copies already synchronized to iCloud or another device are removed. See the data deletion guide for the current app behavior.

Permissions and Apple services

PawFolio asks for optional permissions only when a feature needs them. Depending on the feature you use, this can include:

  • Photos or Camera for pet and journal images.
  • Calendar for events.
  • Location and WeatherKit for weather context.
  • Music for routine playlists.
  • Notifications for reminders and alerts.

You can deny a permission and continue using the parts of PawFolio that do not need it. Data handled by an Apple service is also subject to that service’s permissions and policies.

Sharing, advertising, and analytics

The current free launch version does not require an account, subscription, or purchase to create a pet or save a journal entry. PawFolio does not operate advertising or a separate analytics account for your journal data. This statement must be reviewed if a future release adds third-party services or changes its data practices.

Retention and deletion

You control local PawFolio data through Settings → Storage → Reset Settings and Data. That action is destructive and is described in the data deletion guide. Photos or information that you separately keep in Apple services may be governed by those services’ controls.

Health and care disclaimer

PawFolio is an organizational and memory tool, not a veterinary, medical, diagnostic, or emergency service. Care details and reports should not replace professional veterinary advice.

Legal-site hosting
